!! 盾矮人增益 Shied Dwarf Benefits
@@.indent
当创造盾矮人角色时，你可以从下列增益中挑选。因为这些增益都会替代「矮人武器擅长」，所以你只能选择其中一个

''盾矮人武器擅长：''Shield dwarves spend more time aboveground and in forested areas than their gold dwarf cousins. This has led to them relying on axes as tools and weapons.

增益：你获得手斧和战斧的擅长。此增益替代「矮人武器擅长」

''盾牌擅长：''Besieged by numerous threats such as orcs and goblins, the shield dwarves take the name of their people seriously. Many are trained to use a shield regardless of duty or station.

增益：你获得轻盾的擅长。此增益替代「矮人武器擅长」
